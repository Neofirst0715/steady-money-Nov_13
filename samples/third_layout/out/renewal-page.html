How we counted

  • Started: everyone on the attendance sheet.
  • Took both Money Checks: has a score at both checks named above.
  • Stuck with it: has not missed the last two sessions without telling the counsellor ahead. Arriving late counts as attending. Diaries are not part of this count.
  • Improved: the Money Check rose by 3 points or more between the two checks named above. A rise of 1 or 2 points is shown but not counted, because it could be one good day. This line is proposed and has not yet been confirmed by the counsellors. Nobody without a latest score is counted as improved, and nobody is left out of the totals.
  • The two checks to compare were chosen before the numbers were seen: the first and the latest.

What this page can't tell you

  • Scores are the client's own rating, given face to face with their counsellor.
  • This is a halfway view. The final Money Check is at session 6.
  • There is no written scoring guide, so these numbers should not be compared with another agency's.
  • It covers this one group only.
